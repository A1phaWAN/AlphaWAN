WisGateOS2 2.2.x Release Notes
------------------------------

IMPORTANT UPDATE NOTICE:
This is a significant update of the WisGateOS2 firmware. 
Read these notes carefully. 
Downgrading to earlier versions from WisDM and the local WebUI is NOT possible after this update. 
All extensions for WisGateOS2 2.2.x are digitally signed. Extensions will auto-update during this process. 
Users of the WireGuard Extension should contact RAK support BEFORE updating.
